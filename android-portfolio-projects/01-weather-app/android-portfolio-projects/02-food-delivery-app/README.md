# Feature Project: Food Delivery App

## 1️⃣ Problem Statement
* **What problem will your app solve?**  
  Food delivery apps frequently suffer from confusing menu modifier setups (portion sizes, sides, dietary notes) and unreliable local cart state during browsing. This app delivers a clear item customization engine and an intuitive, friction-free checkout flow.
* **Why does it matter?**  
  Complex checkout forms and hidden pricing details drive high cart-abandonment rates. Streamlining item selection and cart calculations builds customer trust and increases completed orders.

## 2️⃣ Key Features (Top 5)
- Restaurant catalog with multi-criteria filters (Cuisine, Rating, Delivery Time)
- Menu modifier dialog (Toppings, sizes, special instructions)
- Persistent local cart with dynamic taxes, delivery fees, and discount calculations
- Mock checkout workflow with multiple saved delivery addresses
- Multi-stage live order tracking simulation (Received -> Kitchen -> En Route -> Delivered)

## 3️⃣ Tech Stack
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Room DB, Hilt, Coroutines & Flow, Retrofit, MVVM

## 4️⃣ Timeline
