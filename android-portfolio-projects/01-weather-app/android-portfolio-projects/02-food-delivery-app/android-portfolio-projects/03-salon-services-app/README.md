# Professional Project: Salon Services Booking App

## 1️⃣ Problem Statement
* **What problem will your app solve?**  
  Manual booking systems result in phone-tag, schedule overlap, and costly no-shows. For clients, viewing real-time availability for their preferred stylist is difficult. For salon managers, handling simultaneous stylist schedules, service time buffers, and revenue tracking is chaotic.
* **Why does it matter?**  
  Empty chairs and double-booked slots cause direct revenue losses and damage reputation. Automated schedule conflict resolution and instant confirmations boost salon operational capacity and client retention.

## 2️⃣ Key Features (Top 5)
- Real-time stylist slot-booking engine with zero double-booking conflicts
- Categorized service menu with clear pricing and service duration breakdowns
- Full in-app appointment lifecycle management (Book, Reschedule, Cancel)
- Dual-role UI perspectives (Client booking flow vs. Stylist / Manager schedule board)
- Automated appointment notifications, reminders, and digital receipts

## 3️⃣ Tech Stack
Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Firebase Auth / JWT, Ktor / Retrofit, Room DB, Hilt, WorkManager, MVI / MVVM

## 4️⃣ Timeline
25 – 30 Days
